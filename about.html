Who We Are

Our Story

Three decades of hosting Lucknow's most cherished moments.

Since 1994

Thirty-five years in a Mughlai kitchen teaches you patience.

FM began quietly, at the tables of Lucknow, where food and hospitality are not services but a way of life. Over three decades we've grown — but we've never hurried. Our chefs, among the best Awadh has to offer, bring more than thirty-five years of accumulated craft to every degh, every counter, every plate.

Our kitchen has quietly cooked for celebrations across India — some of them, for families whose names are familiar to every household. It is an honour we carry lightly and a standard we carry always.

We pride ourselves on our taste, and the quality of our food.

Why We Stand Apart

Going against the trend.

"We watch the market race toward cheaper. We chose to stay here, where the ingredients and the time are the whole point."

The industry has quietly made a norm of cheaper spice, rushed stock, and mass reheating — and people have grown used to it. We built our name on the opposite instinct. Our deghs stay on burning coals for hours; our workers are paid for two full shifts rather than let the food be hurried. When a guest sits at your table, the flavour reminds them what food was always meant to be.
