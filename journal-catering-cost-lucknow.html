How Much Does a Caterer in Lucknow Cost?

A frame-by-frame breakdown, so no line in any quote surprises you later.

"Catering in Lucknow" is quoted per plate, but few people explain what is inside the plate price. This guide breaks a catering quote down into its real frames — so you can compare two caterers honestly, not just by the headline number.

Frame 1 — The per-plate price

The base number covers a defined menu: a fixed set of starters, a main course with a set number of dums, rice, breads, dal, desserts. It rarely covers live counters, special menus, or premium stations. Ask precisely: how many starters, how many veg & non-veg mains, and what happens if the wedding party runs late?

Frame 2 — Live counters

Live stations are charged separately — a chaat counter, a kebab-parantha stand, a dumpling or ramen station, a chocolate fountain, a kulfi cart. Each carries its own ingredients and its own chef and his own counters are the ones guests photograph, so the cost is real. Get each counter itemised before you sign.

Frame 3 — Staff and service

Service teams — floor captains, servers, and the on-site coordinator — are part of any credible quote. A quote with no service line usually means untrained, unmanageable staff on the day. Ask how many servers per hundred guests.

Frame 4 — Quality and sourcing

The quietest cost difference is the ingredient standard. A caterer who clears coals for hours and pays two shifts for slow-cooked dum is spending money you never see on the page — but taste it in every bite. When a quote looks too good, look for where the corner was cut: reheated curries, frozen paranthas, or oil instead of ghee on the tables.

Frame 5 — The questions that protect you

  • Are they serving vegetarian and non-vegetarian lines separately, without mixing utensils?
  • Who supervises the kitchen on the day?
  • What is the policy if your guest count changes a week before?
  • Are the live counters included or billed separately?
  • Can you taste the menu before paying in full?

Every FM quote is itemised frame by frame — because a clear quote is the first promise. Share your guest count and occasion, and we will give you an honest, written breakdown, no obligation.
